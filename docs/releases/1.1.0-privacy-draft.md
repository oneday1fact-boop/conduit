# Proposed policy updates for CONDUIT 1.1.0 — review before publication

This is a draft for the new optional account and purchase features. It has not replaced the published privacy policy or any Play declaration.

CONDUIT can be played in Daily and Practice modes without signing in. Progress and preferences are stored on the device. Optional rewarded advertising is offered when a player requests a Hint; Pro does not remove these optional ads.

Players who choose Google sign-in allow CONDUIT to use their account identifier and email address to maintain a game account. Signed-in puzzle progress, completion history and Pro purchase association can be stored in the existing Google Firebase project to support restoration on another device. Device preferences remain local. CONDUIT does not receive the player's Google password or full payment-card details.

Pro is a US$2.99 one-time Google Play purchase; local store prices may vary. Google Play handles payment. A separate Cloudflare-hosted verification service receives the Firebase authentication token and purchase token to verify the transaction with Google and associate it with the correct game account. Purchase identifiers, ownership status and verification timestamps are retained in protected server records. Pending or invalid payments do not unlock Pro; refunds may revoke access.

Firebase/Google and Cloudflare process data needed to provide these services. Requests are transmitted using HTTPS. Authentication tokens, private keys and purchase tokens must not be included in diagnostic logs or public app assets. Existing AdMob collection/use disclosures must be retained and reviewed against the actual configured SDK behavior.

Players can request deletion inside Account > Delete account and cloud progress after recent sign-in, or contact oneday1fact@gmail.com. Account deletion removes Firebase sign-in and cloud progress; guest saves on the device remain separate. Deletion does not refund a Play purchase. A temporary security guard prevents stale sessions recreating deleted data, and a non-reversible purchase-token hash may remain to prevent purchase reuse. Moving Pro after deletion requires support.

Before publishing: verify the exact retention periods, free-plan limits, support handling, external deletion-request page, Families/all-ages account requirements, reviewer access and every Data Safety answer. Proposed additional Data Safety categories for review include email address, user ID, gameplay progress/app activity and purchase history, with collection optional where only the optional account/Pro feature requires it. Do not submit these declarations automatically.
