# CONDUIT Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 25, 2026 ## 1. About CONDUIT CONDUIT is a simple English word-puzzle game intended for children, teenagers, and adults. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game. ## 2. Information stored by the game CONDUIT may store game progress and preferences on your device so that your progress and settings can be retained between sessions. The game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other personal details to play. ## 3. Advertising and third-party services CONDUIT uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded advertisements. When an advertisement is requested or viewed, Google and its advertising services may automatically collect or process information such as: - IP address and approximate location derived from it. - Device and advertising identifiers, where permitted. - Ad views, taps, and other interactions with the app or advertisements. - Diagnostic and performance information. Google uses information according to its own policies and settings for purposes that may include delivering ads, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and maintaining its services. Data handling can depend on the device, consent, and applicable settings. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Google AdMob information: https://support.google.com/admob/ ## 4. Children's privacy CONDUIT is intended for a broad audience that includes children. Advertising and information handling must comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements. Advertising settings and safeguards will be configured as required for the intended audience. Parents or guardians who have questions about children's privacy may contact the developer using the details below. ## 5. Data sharing The developer does not sell personal information collected directly from players. Information may be processed by Google and its service providers as described above to provide advertising and related services. ## 6. Data retention and security Game progress and preferences may remain stored locally on your device until they are cleared or the app's data is removed. Information handled by Google is subject to Google's retention and security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. Your choices You can stop using the game or clear its locally stored data through your device settings. You can also review available advertising and privacy controls in your device settings and Google's services. Parents or guardians may contact the developer with questions or requests concerning children's information. ## 8. Changes to this policy This policy may be updated if the game or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page. ## 9.
