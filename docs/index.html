CONDUIT Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Developer: oneday1fact

Request account and data deletion · Contact support

1. About CONDUIT

CONDUIT is an English word-puzzle game intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. Daily and Practice can be played without an account. Optional Google sign-in enables cloud progress and restoration of a CONDUIT Pro purchase.

2. Information we handle

  • On your device: puzzle answers, completion history, elapsed time, hint usage, Daily streak and preferences such as sound, music, theme and haptics.
  • Optional account: Google/Firebase account identifier, email address and basic profile information supplied by Google, such as a display name or profile image. Firebase Authentication maintains the sign-in account; CONDUIT does not receive your Google password.
  • Cloud progress: signed-in puzzle progress, completion history, timing, hint usage and update timestamps, stored with your account identifier to sync progress between devices.
  • Pro purchases: Google Play purchase tokens, product identifier, account association, entitlement and verification timestamps. Google Play handles payment; CONDUIT does not receive your payment-card details. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and does not remove optional rewarded Hint ads.
  • Service and advertising data: network requests reveal an IP address to the receiving service. Google AdMob may collect approximate location inferred from IP, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, ad/app interactions and diagnostic/performance information.

3. Purposes and service providers

Account and progress information authenticate players and save and restore progress between devices. Purchase information verifies, acknowledges and restores Pro, checks refunds and prevents purchase-token reuse. Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud storage; a Cloudflare-hosted service verifies purchases with Google Play and handles account deletion.

AdMob processes advertising data to deliver optional rewarded ads, measure performance, maintain services and prevent fraud. Ads are requested when you choose a Hint. Data handling depends on device, consent and SDK settings. We do not sell account, progress or purchase information. Service providers process data to supply these features; advertising data is handled as described by Google.

4. Retention and security

Local progress remains until cleared using device controls. Signing out does not erase guest progress. Cloud account, progress and purchase records are retained while needed to provide your account, sync and purchase restoration, unless you request deletion. Provider diagnostic and advertising records follow their applicable retention practices.

Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Cloud progress is restricted to its account owner; the backend controls purchase entitlements. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

5. Delete your CONDUIT account and data

You can request deletion without reinstalling the game. Email oneday1fact@gmail.com with the subject CONDUIT account deletion, preferably from the email used to sign in. State whether you want your account and cloud progress deleted, or only your cloud progress deleted.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, authentication codes or purchase tokens. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing your request.

In the app: open the Account icon, sign in, choose Delete account and cloud progress, then confirm. A recent sign-in is required; sign out and sign back in if requested.

Account deletion removes the Firebase sign-in identity, cloud progress, settings, entitlement and account-to-purchase link from CONDUIT's active account records. In-app requests are processed by the service when confirmed. Email requests are reviewed and completed after ownership verification; contact support for an update.

A temporary deleted-account identifier is kept for approximately two hours, then removed by scheduled cleanup, to prevent stale sessions recreating data. A one-way hash of a previously linked purchase token and deletion marker can remain for fraud prevention; that retained record no longer contains the account identifier or raw purchase token. Provider backups and records outside CONDUIT follow their own retention practices.

Deletion does not delete your Google account, erase separate guest saves or device copies, or automatically refund Google Play purchases. Clear app data on each device to remove local copies. Contact support before deleting your account if you need help preserving or moving Pro access.

6. Your choices

You may play Daily and Practice without signing in and choose not to request rewarded ads. You may sign out, request account or cloud-data deletion, and manage advertising identifiers and privacy controls through Android and Google settings. Cloud sync, sign-in, purchases and ads require a connection.

7. Children's privacy

CONDUIT is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek account information from children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied account information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

9. Contact
